Once upon a time, in a world not so different from ours, we started our journey selling robot puppets. Yes, you heard it right, robot puppets! They were the talk of the town, with their shiny metallic bodies and cute little puppet strings.

But one day, as we were playing with our robot puppets, a bolt of lightning struck our workshop. Sparks flew, smoke filled the room, and when it cleared, we saw our puppets had come to life! They were no longer just puppets, they were real, living robots!

From that day forward, we knew we were destined for something bigger. We started building real robots, not just puppets. Our robots could dance, sing, and even make a mean cup of coffee. They were the perfect companions for any task!

Today, we're known as the creators of the famous Muraho Robots. But we'll never forget our humble beginnings with those robot puppets. So come, join us on this wild ride, and let's make the world a more fun and robotic place together!
